# Embryo Telemetry

Embryo Telemetry is Embryo-Zero's fork of [opentelemetry-collector](https://github.com/open-telemetry/opentelemetry-collector) (c5ff2f3, commit c5ff2f3). It does standard collector for metrics, logs, traces.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
